05 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
plug in.

Still taking shape.
Already thoughtfully considered.

When can I get MirrorPhone?+

MirrorPhone is in development. Follow the launch project on GitHub for release updates.

Which devices and Macs will it support?+

MirrorPhone is built for macOS, with iPhone and iPad connected over USB. Tested compatibility details will accompany the release.

Can I control my phone from my Mac?+

Interact directly on your iPhone or iPad. MirrorPhone displays and captures its screen on your Mac; remote touch control is outside the current scope.

Does it work wirelessly?+

The upcoming app uses a USB data cable. Wireless mirroring is outside the current release plan.

What can I save?+

Save PNG screenshots or H.264/AAC MP4 recordings, with a frame or just the screen. Device audio can be included when enabled and available. Screen-only PNGs preserve the source pixels; recordings use a fixed portrait or landscape canvas.
